# SSEC Design Principles

## 1. Engineered Clarity

Every element communicates its purpose with precision — like well-documented code.

**This means:** Navigation is obvious, content hierarchy is immediate, interactions are predictable. Information architecture mirrors the logical structure of software: modular, composable, and dependency-aware.

**This does NOT mean:** The design is sterile or cold. Clarity enables warmth — when users don't struggle to find things, they can focus on the substance.

## 2. Open by Default

The design reflects SSEC's commitment to open source and transparency.

**This means:** Content is accessible without barriers. Data is shown, not hidden. The site itself could serve as a reference implementation. Design decisions are documented. Technical depth is welcomed, not dumbed down.

**This does NOT mean:** Everything is shown at once. Progressive disclosure is still essential — complexity is available, not imposed.

## 3. Research-Grade Rigor

Design decisions are as defensible as peer-reviewed research.

**This means:** Every color passes WCAG AA contrast. Every font size is mathematically derived. Every spacing value sits on a consistent scale. Accessibility is a requirement, not an afterthought. UW brand guidelines are followed precisely.

**This does NOT mean:** The design is conservative or afraid to be distinctive. Rigor enables boldness — when the foundation is sound, creative choices are confident.

## 4. AI-Native Thinking

The site embodies the intelligence and dynamism of the AI systems SSEC builds.

**This means:** Interactions feel responsive and intelligent. Data visualizations reveal patterns. The interface adapts and rewards exploration. Subtle motion suggests computation and discovery. The experience feels like it was built by people who understand AI deeply.

**This does NOT mean:** Gratuitous animations, chatbot popups, or AI buzzword decoration. The AI feel comes from the intelligence of the design itself, not from slapping "AI" labels on everything.

## 5. Boundless Discovery

Inspired by UW's "Be Boundless" ethos — the site invites exploration across disciplines.

**This means:** Cross-linking between projects, people, and publications creates a knowledge graph users can traverse. Each page offers paths to go deeper. The interdisciplinary nature of SSEC's work is visible in how content connects.

**This does NOT mean:** Users are lost in a maze. Every journey has clear waypoints and the ability to orient back to familiar ground.
